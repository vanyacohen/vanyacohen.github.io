    <p>
      I am a PhD Student at the
      <a href="https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~ml/">University of Texas at Austin</a>, advised by <a href="https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~mooney/">Ray Mooney</a>. I am also a Research Scientist at <a href="https://blackbird.ai">Blackbird.AI</a>, where I created <a href="https://blackbird.ai/compass-context/">Compass</a>, an agentic research and analysis application for multimodal social media content.
    </p>
    <p>
      <b>Research:</b> My research centers on grounded natural language processing, reinforcement learning, and robotics. Broadly, I am interested in creating AI systems that can understand and interact with complex environments through language and action.
    </p>
    <p>
      <b>Background:</b> I completed my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Computer Science at
      <a href="https://cs.brown.edu">Brown University</a>, where I was advised by Stefanie Tellex and George Konidaris in the
      <a href="https://h2r.cs.brown.edu">Humans to Robots Lab</a>.
      Before my PhD, I worked at <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luminoso">Luminoso</a> in Boston, contributing to <a href="https://conceptnet.io">ConceptNet</a>.
    </p>
    <p>
      <b>Notable work:</b> I co-created <a href="https://www.wired.com/story/dangerous-ai-open-source/">OpenGPT-2</a>, the first publicly released open-source LLM, and the <a href="https://huggingface.co/datasets/Skylion007/openwebtext">OpenWebText</a> dataset (4M+ downloads). My <a href="https://www.csauthors.net/distance/vanya-cohen/paul-erdos">Erdos number is 3</a>.
